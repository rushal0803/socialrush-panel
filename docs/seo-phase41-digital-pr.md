# Phase 41 — Backlinks & Digital PR

## Goal

Earn relevant editorial citations to useful SocialRUSH resources without buying rankings, manufacturing placements, or forcing exact-match commercial anchors.

## What Phase 41 promotes

The canonical outreach registry lives in `lib/seo/digital-pr.ts`. It intentionally prioritizes free tools, checklists, audits and safety guidance rather than transactional money pages.

Current asset categories:

- planning calculators and checklists;
- measurement utilities;
- campaign/audit resources;
- account-access and public-link safety guidance.

## Outreach workflow

1. Start with one useful resource and one specific audience.
2. Qualify the publisher, educator, newsletter or community for genuine topical fit.
3. Explain why the resource adds value to an existing lesson, article, roundup or reader question.
4. Do not request a forced exact-match anchor or sitewide link.
5. Disclose SocialRUSH affiliation when contributing on behalf of the brand.
6. Treat a backlink or media mention as verified only after the third-party page is independently checked.
7. Never record an outreach email, promised placement or verbal agreement as a live backlink.

## Quality exclusions

Do not use:

- paid dofollow placements intended to manipulate rankings;
- PBNs or mass directory submission;
- automated blog-comment or forum-profile links;
- irrelevant guest posts;
- reciprocal link schemes at scale;
- fabricated awards, quotes, customer stories or media logos;
- promises that links will guarantee traffic, rankings, leads or revenue.

## Measurement

Phase 41 code tracks outreach-ready assets and guardrails, not external backlink totals.

When verified external data becomes available, measure separately:

- referring pages and domains;
- topical relevance;
- linked asset and natural anchor;
- publication date;
- referral traffic;
- assisted conversions where attribution is available;
- indexation/crawl visibility of the linking page.

Search Console, analytics or a verified backlink provider should be the source of those external measurements. Do not infer them from outreach activity.

## Search evidence and legitimate acquisition

On 2026-11-16, read-only `gsc_wizard_list_sites` returned `payment_required` / `subscription_required`. No subscription, paid API, property change or indexing request was made. This is a data-access blocker, not evidence that clicks/rankings are zero. No such export was supplied for this audit; performance analysis remains BLOCKED. Do not join these exports to personal order destinations.

Preserve existing canonical ownership, useful guides and money-page URLs. Prioritize existing Instagram Followers India/Likes, YouTube, LinkedIn and Facebook routes for technical correctness and transparent terms. Do not mass-publish repetitive landing pages, buy links or fabricate reviews/case-study outcomes. Proposed sustainable editorial work: account optimization, audience/content planning, platform analytics, accessibility, fraud/security guidance and genuine service-policy explanations. Pair each article with a verified user need, a distinctive practical resource, clear author/review dates and relevant existing internal links. Measure only observed outcomes through approved sources.

Artificial engagement, guaranteed delivery or misleading follower-quality claims can conflict with platform anti-manipulation rules and consumer expectations. This task does not implement new fake-engagement fulfillment, scraping or deceptive offers. Before any commercial release, an accountable owner/legal reviewer must compare actual supplier behavior, claims, refund/refill eligibility and disclosures against current applicable platform policies and consumer law. Existing source/static tests are not legal approval or proof of supplier quality. Treat approval as unresolved, not an excuse to assert compliance.

## Existing CRM and automation

The repository already contains CRM pipeline/autopilot, recovery queue, support/refill, order notification, Resend webhook and scheduled email/prospect/notification routes. Reuse these surfaces instead of creating a second CRM. Existing `test:crm-pipeline` and `test:order-notifications` checks exercise policy/retry/suppression contracts offline. No campaign, scrape, external worker, scheduler or email was triggered.

Activation remains untouched: production state is not independently known. Before any separate approval, prove job scope, provider destinations, deduplication keys, bounded retry/dead-letter behavior, operational observability, cancellation/suppression, feedback/refill eligibility, consent provenance and unsubscribe/retention requirements. Necessary transactional records must remain distinct from optional promotions. Support organization and read-only order visibility should precede promotional automation. Do not enable automatic promotional messages, unsolicited scraping or bulk outreach.

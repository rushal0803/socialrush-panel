# Phase 5R — Social Media Services India Intent

## Goal

Capture the broad commercial query family around **social media services India** without creating a duplicate doorway page or presenting SocialRUSH as a full-service social media management agency.

## SERP finding

Live search review on 28 September 2026 shows this query family is mixed with social media marketing agencies and boosting providers. SocialRUSH should therefore own the narrower order-based service interpretation.

## Canonical ownership

The existing canonical owner remains:

`/services`

New alias:

`/social-media-services-india` → `/services`

No new indexable page is created.

## Visible authority content

The Services hub now explains:

- audience services,
- content activity services,
- agency and repeat-client workflows,
- public-link ordering,
- live checkout facts,
- and the distinction from monthly social media management.

## Guardrails

- No claims of being a social media management agency.
- No guarantee of reach, leads, sales, monetization or revenue.
- No duplicate commercial URL.
- Current checkout price, quantity, delivery, refill and payment facts remain authoritative.

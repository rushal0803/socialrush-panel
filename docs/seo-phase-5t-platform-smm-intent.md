# Phase 5T — Platform SMM Panel India Intent

## Goal

Capture platform-level commercial discovery queries such as **Instagram SMM panel India**, **YouTube SMM panel India**, **LinkedIn SMM panel India**, **Facebook SMM panel India**, **Twitter / X SMM panel India**, **TikTok SMM panel India**, and **Telegram SMM panel India** without creating thin doorway pages.

## SERP finding — 28 September 2026

Live search results show platform-level SMM panel pages ranking as multi-service hubs. They commonly group followers/subscribers, likes, views, comments and related services under one platform query rather than one service-specific transaction.

## Canonical ownership

Existing platform hubs remain canonical:

- Instagram → `/instagram-growth-india`
- YouTube → `/youtube-growth-india`
- LinkedIn → `/linkedin-growth-india`
- Facebook → `/facebook-growth-india`
- Twitter / X → `/x-growth-india`
- TikTok → `/tiktok-growth-india`
- Telegram → `/services/telegram`

Query-shaped aliases permanently redirect to those owners. No new indexable landing page is created.

## Visible authority content

Each participating platform hub now explains what users should compare before ordering:

- current INR pricing and quantity totals,
- public-link requirements,
- India-friendly payment flow,
- delivery/refill/support terms,
- the actual service paths available for that platform.

## Guardrails

- No “best,” “#1,” or “cheapest” claim.
- No guaranteed reach, ranking, sales, leads, monetization or platform outcome.
- Live service and checkout facts remain authoritative.
- Existing service-specific transactional pages keep their own intent.

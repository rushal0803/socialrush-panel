# Phase 46 — ₹5L Monthly Revenue Engine

Phase 46 connects the existing verified revenue, traffic, packages, retention, agency/reseller and referral systems into one monthly revenue control loop.

## Target

- Monthly roadmap target: ₹5,00,000 net revenue.
- The target is not a forecast or guarantee.
- Month-to-date revenue is built only from verified paid/completed orders minus completed refunds.

## Target arithmetic

The engine computes:

- Current-month verified gross and net revenue.
- ₹5L progress and remaining gap.
- Calendar-month target pace in Asia/Kolkata.
- Required daily net revenue for the remaining days.
- Current verified AOV.
- Whole-month and remaining order counts at the current AOV.
- Reference order count at the existing ₹1,500 AOV operating target.
- Repeat-order revenue contribution.
- Multi-order customer rate.
- Leading platform revenue share.

These are arithmetic and verified historical measurements, not predictions.

## Growth levers

The engine ranks existing systems rather than inventing a new sales mechanism:

1. Revenue pace and verified revenue analytics.
2. AOV through current quantities and packages.
3. Repeat revenue through the existing retention/reactivation engine.
4. Qualified acquisition through the Phase 45 traffic engine.
5. Agency/reseller recurring requirements through existing client-plan workflows.
6. Platform concentration monitoring using verified paid-order revenue.

## Safety

Phase 46 does not:

- Change service prices or package discounts.
- Change wallet balances or payable amounts.
- Change payment verification or order submission.
- Auto-place, auto-renew or auto-charge orders.
- Invent revenue, traffic, conversion lift, discounts, availability or delivery results.
- Treat the 100K traffic target as a revenue forecast.

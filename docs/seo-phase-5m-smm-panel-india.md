# Phase 5M — SMM Panel India authority intent

## Search opportunity

Live India SERPs for SMM-panel queries emphasize a recurring set of decision factors: INR pricing, UPI/local payments, platform coverage, quantity limits, delivery/refill information, order tracking and agency/reseller workflows. SocialRUSH already owns a broad commercial comparison route at `/services`, so creating another indexable `/smm-panel-india` page would unnecessarily split authority.

## Canonical decision

- `/services` remains the single canonical owner for cross-platform social-media service comparison.
- `/smm-panel-india` and `/services/smm-panel-india` redirect to `/services`.
- No new doorway or near-duplicate landing page was created.

## Page improvements

The Services page now:
- targets “SMM Panel India” in the title, description, keyword model and visible hero;
- explains what an SMM panel is without claiming SocialRUSH is “best”, “#1” or “cheapest”;
- exposes comparison criteria for pricing/limits, payment methods, public-link requirements, delivery/refill and tracking;
- renders current platform coverage from the live service catalog;
- explains India-friendly checkout using the existing UPI, Bank Transfer and USDT payment-intent model;
- links naturally to pricing, trust, packages and agency workflows.

## Integrity controls

- Live service availability remains catalog-authoritative.
- Payment options shown at checkout remain authoritative.
- No delivery guarantee, rank guarantee or unsupported quality claim was added.
- Existing canonical ownership and SEO health monitoring now include the short `/smm-panel-india` alias.

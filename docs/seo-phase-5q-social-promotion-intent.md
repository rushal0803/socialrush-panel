# Phase 5Q — Social Media Promotion Services India Intent

## Goal

Capture the cross-platform search family around **social media promotion services India** without creating another doorway or duplicate commercial URL.

## Evidence reviewed

Live SERP review on 28 September 2026 showed the phrase "social media promotion services India" is mixed-intent:

- some results are full social-media management agencies,
- some are digital/content promotion providers,
- some are audience and engagement service providers.

SocialRUSH is not a full monthly social-media management agency. The correct fit is the order-based promotion interpretation: eligible follower, subscriber, member, like, view, comment, save and share campaigns using public social-media destinations.

## Canonical decision

The existing canonical owner remains:

`/social-media-growth-india`

No new indexable promotion page is created.

Legacy/query-shape aliases redirect permanently to the canonical hub:

- `/social-media-promotion-services-india`
- `/social-media-promotion-service-india`

This preserves one owner for the cross-platform growth, engagement and promotion family.

## Visible authority content

The growth hub now includes a dedicated promotion-services section that explains:

1. audience promotion,
2. post/video promotion,
3. public-link campaign requirements,
4. what SocialRUSH does not provide under this term.

The copy explicitly distinguishes order-based growth campaigns from:

- monthly content creation,
- community management,
- organic strategy execution,
- paid-ad management retainers.

## Guardrails

- No claims of guaranteed reach, leads, sales, monetization or revenue.
- No "best", "#1" or "cheapest" claims.
- No new doorway page.
- No changes to checkout, pricing, service catalog, orders, payments or database logic.
- Existing canonical freshness and IndexNow release path are reused because the canonical hub is materially updated on 28 September 2026.

## Monitoring

The SEO health monitor now verifies both promotion aliases redirect to `/social-media-growth-india`.

Once Search Console access is restored, measure query/page performance for:

- social media promotion services India
- social media promotion service India
- social media promotion India
- social media post promotion services India
- social media audience promotion India

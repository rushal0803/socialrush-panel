# Phase 39 — India Expansion

## Objective

Expand SocialRUSH's India relevance without creating thin city/state doorway pages or competing with the national keyword owners established in Phase 26.

## Current strategy

The canonical regional discovery path remains:

- `/social-media-growth-india`

Major India market clusters are represented inside that authority hub. Regional discovery does **not** create separate pricing, delivery, refill, office, team, or service-availability claims.

## Regional clusters

- Delhi NCR
- Mumbai & Pune
- Bengaluru & Hyderabad
- Chennai & South India
- Kolkata & East India
- Ahmedabad & Surat
- Jaipur & Rajasthan
- Chandigarh & Punjab

## When a dedicated city/state page may be created

A new location URL requires all of the following:

1. Verified query demand from Search Console or another reliable source.
2. A distinct user intent that is not already owned by a national service or platform hub.
3. Unique regional information beyond city-name substitution.
4. A clear internal-link role that strengthens rather than competes with national canonicals.

## Prohibited patterns

- Cloning a national money page and changing only a city/state name.
- Unsupported local-office or local-team claims.
- Repeating the same price, delivery, refill, and checkout copy across many location URLs.

## Validation

`npm run seo:india-expansion` verifies the regional registry, India hub integration, internal discovery link, and absence of unapproved city doorway routes.

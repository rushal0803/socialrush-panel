## Best pages to promote

1. `/tools/social-media-growth-budget-calculator` — a transparent planning calculator that uses only visitor-entered values, so it does not pretend to publish market-rate research.
2. `/tools/creator-growth-checklist` — a concise, interactive preparation resource for profile readiness, content, tracking, budgeting, public URLs, and account safety.
3. `/tools/instagram-engagement-rate-calculator` — explains its formula and processes user-entered metrics in the browser.
4. `/tools/utm-link-builder` — a practical free workflow tool for campaign measurement.
5. `/blog/why-public-link-ordering-is-safer` — a safety-oriented explanatory guide that reinforces not sharing passwords, OTPs, or recovery codes.

## Suitable audiences and outreach angles

- Creator and social-media educator blogs: a free checklist or measurement tool readers can use alongside their publishing advice.
- Digital marketing and small-business resources: a transparent campaign-budget planning worksheet; no claimed pricing benchmarks.
- Indian creator newsletters and marketing publications: a locally relevant creator-tool collection with browser-first privacy messaging.
- Resource pages, course materials, and community roundups: useful calculators and UTM tools that complement planning lessons.

Lead with the utility, method, and limitation of the resource. Offer a short factual description and a suggested contextual placement; do not request sitewide links, paid dofollow placements, PBN placements, comment links, mass-directory submissions, automated backlink campaigns, or irrelevant guest posts.

## Anchor-text diversity

Use varied, natural anchors such as: “creator growth checklist”, “social media budget planner”, “Instagram engagement calculator”, “campaign UTM builder”, “planning resource for creators”, “free social-media tool”, and the brand name “SocialRUSH”. Avoid repeatedly forcing commercial exact-match anchors.
